SharePoint will not run this app from Documents
================================================

When you open the file in SharePoint and it downloads, then
goes blank, that is SharePoint blocking HTML. Embed cannot fix it.

STOP using these in Embed:
  .../Shared Documents/capacity-main/index.html
  .../Shared Documents/capacity-main/CapacityTracker.html
  .../Shared Documents/capacity-main/index.aspx


USE THE APP IN ONE OF THESE TWO WAYS
------------------------------------

1. On your computer (works right now)
   After SharePoint downloads CapacityTracker.html, open your
   Downloads folder and double-click that file.
   It should open in Edge or Chrome and stay open.

2. As a real website (for the SharePoint page)
   The tracker is also published here:

   https://jmwilson92.github.io/capacity/

   Open that in a new tab first. If you see the full app,
   put THIS in the SharePoint Embed box:

<iframe src="https://jmwilson92.github.io/capacity/" width="100%" height="1000" style="border:0;"></iframe>

   If Embed blocks github.io, skip Embed. Add a Quick links
   item that points at the same https://jmwilson92.github.io/capacity/
   address. People click it and the app opens.


NOTE
----
Data is saved in the browser (this computer), not in SharePoint
Documents. That is expected until IT hosts it inside your tenant.
